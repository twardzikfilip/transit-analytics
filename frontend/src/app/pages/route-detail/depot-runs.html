Depot runs

Runs that leave the depot area for the route (pull-out) or return to it (pull-in): a brigade's first or last run, or the run before or after a break of 30 minutes or more, when it starts or ends away from the standard end stops. A bus waiting long at a standard end stop is on a break there, not at the depot.
