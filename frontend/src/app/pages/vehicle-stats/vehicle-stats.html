How these are calculated
  • Hours in service come from the recorded positions (every 20 seconds): each report on a line counts until the vehicle's next report, up to a minute, so gaps in the feed don't count as service.
  • Days are service days, 03:00 to 03:00, so a night run belongs to the evening before.
  • Left their brigade: the vehicle's last report on its brigade that day came while the timetable had the brigade running (mid-run or in a short layover; not at a break of 30 minutes or more, and not in the last 15 minutes of its day), compared with where the vehicle was in its schedule (report time minus its delay). Switched: it served another line later that day; disappeared: it wasn't seen again. Changing brigade on the same line doesn't count, and neither do gaps in recording.
  • Not used at all: the fleet is every bus ever seen on the live feed; a bus counts as used when it reported a line in the window. Its last time in service is its last report on a line before the end of the window.
  • Operators: all operators' vehicles are counted unless the menu at the top limits them; only brigades in the city timetable (BKM) can be checked against it. A brigade taken over by another operator's vehicle still shows who took it over.
