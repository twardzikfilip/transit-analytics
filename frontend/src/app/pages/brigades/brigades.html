How this is worked out
  • Scheduled: the brigade's trips in the timetable for the service day (03:00 to 03:00).
  • Actual: the recorded positions (every 10 s) of the buses that ran the brigade, matched to its trips as on Abnormal trips. A bus's part of a trip runs from the first stop it was seen heading to, to the last; its times are the scheduled ones there plus the delay the feed reported.
  • Outcome is the one Abnormal trips gives. Not run parts are those of trips lost, missed, cancelled or turned short; not seen parts are where the bus stopped reporting but probably carried on. Not recorded: a gap in recording then.
  • On time setting off: between 1 minute early and 3 minutes late.
