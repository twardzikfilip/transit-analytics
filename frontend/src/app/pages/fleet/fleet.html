How these are calculated
  • The fleet is every bus ever seen on the live feed. A bus is in use when it reported a line in the window.
  • Hours in service come from the recorded positions (every 10 seconds): each report on a line counts until the bus's next report, up to a minute, so gaps in the feed don't count as service. Days are service days, 03:00 to 03:00.
  • Broken down, as on Abnormal trips: a trip run in full by two buses of the brigade, one taking over midway, where the replaced bus wasn't used again that day. It is back at its next report on a line after that day.
  • Runs are consecutive reports on one line, brigade and headsign, at most 5 minutes apart; a gap of 30 minutes or more between runs is a break.
  • Abnormal trips of a bus: trips it broke down on, and trips of its brigade it missed (not run while it ran the brigade's other trips that day). A cancelled trip (no bus on the brigade all day) has no bus to show it on. Only the city timetable's (BKM) lines can be judged.
