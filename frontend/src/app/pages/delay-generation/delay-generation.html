How delay generation is measured
  • Delay is the feed's (the operator's figure against the timetable), taken from the first report after a bus leaves each stop: the next stop it reports moves on by one. Reports come every 20 seconds, so one bus's figure is good to about ±20 seconds; averages over many buses are much closer.
  • A stretch's change is the delay on leaving the next stop minus the delay on leaving this one: the drive between them and the time standing at the next stop. Stretches where a report skipped a stop aren't measured, nor are delays over 30 minutes (feed errors). The last stretch of a run (arriving at the terminus) can't be measured.
  • Per day figures are over the days with recordings, and a stretch is shown once it was measured 5 times in the chosen part of the day. Only lines in the city timetable count.
  • Gained per run is the change from a run's first measured stop to its last (runs of 5 stops or more).
