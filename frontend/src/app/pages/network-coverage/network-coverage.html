How this is worked out

  • The city limits, districts and woods come from OpenStreetMap ({{ d.sources.shapesFetched }}). Areas are measured on a grid of {{ d.cellMeters }} m squares, so overlapping woods count once.
  • Stops are those of the city's lines in the current timetable. One near the border also covers the city side of it.
  • Residents of each district are those on the city's register at the end of 2025, for permanent or temporary stay, from the city's report (Raport o stanie Miasta Białegostoku za 2025 rok, table 4): {{ people(totals().people) }} together. Statistics Poland puts the city at {{ people(d.officialPopulation) }}: it counts everyone living here, registered or not, but has no figures by district. So the districts' densities are lower than the city's official one.
  • Around the city, residents come from the gmina office for gmina Juchnowiec Kościelny (end of 2023, from its revitalisation programme) and from the 2021 census elsewhere; a few from Polish Wikipedia, with their year. Hover the date for the source. Places without a figure are left white. Areas there are measured on {{ d.belt.cellMeters }} m squares, outside the city limits.
