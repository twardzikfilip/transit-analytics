How this is worked out

  • The city limits, districts, villages and woods come from OpenStreetMap ({{ d.sources.shapesFetched }}). Areas are measured on a grid of {{ d.cellMeters }} m squares in the city and {{ d.belt.cellMeters }} m squares around it, so overlapping woods count once.
  • Stops are those of the city's lines in the current timetable; one near the border also covers the city side of it. Distances are as the crow flies: walking along streets is longer.
  • Residents of each district are those on the city's register at the end of 2025, for permanent or temporary stay ({{ int(totals().people) }} together). Statistics Poland puts the city at {{ int(d.official.population) }}: it counts everyone living here, registered or not, but has no figures by district. So the districts' densities are lower than the city's official one.
  • Around the city, residents come from the gmina office for gmina Juchnowiec Kościelny (end of 2023) and from the 2021 census elsewhere; a few from Polish Wikipedia, with their year. Hover the date for the source. Places without a figure are left white.
