# places/: reference data about places

Everything the app knows about the city and its surroundings that isn't a timetable or a recording: borders, woods,
bus lanes and residents. The data lives here, not in code. Settings (distances, thresholds) are in `map/*.yml`.

## Changing a figure without rebuilding

Put a file with the same path under `./config/places/`, next to the jar or where the app is started, for example
`./config/places/population/belt.csv`. It replaces the built-in one at the next start (`geo/PlaceData.java`).

## Files

| File | What it is | Source | Edited how |
|---|---|---|---|
| `osm/bialystok.json` | The city border (relation 2910925), its 29 districts (osiedla), the woods within it (`landuse=forest`, `natural=wood`) and, under `valley`, the uninhabited valley of the river Biała where it is wide (one feature per stretch) | OpenStreetMap via Overpass; date in its `fetched` field | Generated: `tools/geo/build_bialystok_geo.py`; the valley's stretches and rules are in `tools/geo/biala_valley.py` |
| `osm/belt.json` | The villages (sołectwa) and towns reaching within 5 km of the city limits, 9 km from Supraśl round the south to Baciuty, with gmina and Wikidata item; the woods and the open land (fields, meadows, ...) in them, pieces of at least 1 ha | OpenStreetMap via Overpass; where it has no landuse, ESA WorldCover 2021 (CC BY 4.0). Nov 2026 | Generated: `tools/geo/build_belt_geo.py`; the rules for the land are in `tools/geo/build_belt_land.py` |
| `osm/bus-lanes.json` | The city's bus lanes | OpenStreetMap via Overpass | Generated by the app (`POST /api/bus-lanes/refresh`); the refreshed copy is saved to `./config/map/bus-lanes.json` (see `map/bus-lanes.yml`) |
| `osm/railways.json` | The railways in use (lines and spurs) in the city and up to 10 km from its limits (towards Łapy, Knyszyn, Czarna Białostocka and Lewickie: up to there) | OpenStreetMap via Overpass, 25 Nov 2026 | Generated: `tools/geo/build_railways.py` |
| `osm/city-rail-proposal.json` | City Rail proposal no. 1: the five railways from Białystok station to Klepacze, Fasty, Lewickie, Wasilków and Kuriany (track points), with their stations and distances along the track: those served today (with their timetable id), Białystok Fabryczny and Kuriany (no trains today), and the six new ones, each placed 100 m past its street crossing | OpenStreetMap via Overpass (railways and streets), 27 Nov 2026 | Generated: `tools/geo/build_city_rail_proposal.py` (the stations and crossings are listed there) |
| `population/districts.csv` | Residents of each district at the end of 2025: permanent and temporary stay | City register, *Raport o stanie Miasta Białegostoku za 2025 rok*, table 4 | By hand |
| `population/belt.csv` | Residents of each village and town of the belt, with the date and source of each figure; empty where none was found | Gmina Juchnowiec Kościelny (31.12.2023), the 2021 census (via Wikidata or polskawliczbach.pl or citypopulation.de), a few Polish Wikipedia figures (2011+) | By hand; first written by `tools/geo/init_belt_population.py` |
| `population/city.csv` | The city's population by Statistics Poland (everyone living here, registered or not) | GUS, via the same city report, table 2 | By hand |

Who uses them:
- **`osm/bialystok.json`:**
  - `CityBoundaries`: the city limits and centre, for the maps, line classification and the zone of a stop;
  - the Network coverage page, which also leaves the Biała valley out like the woods.
- **Network coverage page only (`service/PlaceReferenceData.java`):** `osm/belt.json` and the population files.
- **`osm/bus-lanes.json`:** `buslanes/BusLanes`.
- **`osm/railways.json`:** the City Rail page (`service/CityRailService`).
- **`osm/city-rail-proposal.json`:** City Rail proposal no. 1 (`service/CityRailProposal`).

## The CSV format

- Lines starting with `#` say where the figures come from; the page shows them.
- Then a header line, then one row per place.
- Fields with commas are in double quotes.
- Rows are read by column name, so columns can be added.
- Names in `districts.csv` must match the district names in `osm/bialystok.json`.
- Rows in `belt.csv` are matched by the `osm` column, the place's OpenStreetMap relation.

## Rebuilding the shapes

```sh
tools/geo/fetch_osm.sh                 # Overpass queries → tools/geo/raw/ (not committed); or name files: valley.json
python3 tools/geo/build_bialystok_geo.py
python3 tools/geo/worldcover.py          # the satellites' land cover
python3 tools/geo/build_belt_geo.py
# only for a new belt (other villages); it won't overwrite hand edits without --force:
python3 tools/geo/init_belt_population.py
```

The inputs for the belt's residents are in `tools/geo/sources/`:
- the gmina's table;
- the Wikidata answer;
- the figures from polskawliczbach.pl and Polish Wikipedia;
- `census-2021.json`: the 2021 census per place of the gminas around the city, from citypopulation.de (GUS data).
